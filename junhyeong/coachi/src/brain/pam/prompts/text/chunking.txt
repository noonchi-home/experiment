You turn a household robot's observation record into chunks of what the person does.

# Input
- Record: the robot's observation of the person, one line every {line_step}
(`HH:MM:SS | sentence`) in time order. A line describes the whole scene: the person's
posture, what they are doing, and things lying around. One activity usually fills many
lines in a row, and the wording changes from line to line.

# Task
Go through the record in order and split it into chunks, one chunk per activity.
- Lines in a row that describe the same activity, even in other words, are one chunk.
Its start is the time of its first line and its end the time of its last line. A line
that does not mention the activity but does not show a different one either stays in
the chunk.
- Whenever the person starts doing something else, start a new chunk, however short.
Short actions such as picking something up, putting something down, opening something,
standing up, sitting down, taking a drink, or finishing something are chunks of their
own, even if they fill only one line. Never fold different activities into one.
- A line may still say what was done a moment ago ("has put the book down, and is now
folding towels"). That earlier action belongs to the chunk where it happened; the line belongs
to the chunk of what goes on now.
- When the person is not visible, that is a chunk too. Anything heard while they are
away is a chunk of its own at the time of its line.
- Write each chunk as one short sentence: what the person does, and the things nearby
that matter for it, such as something that was just emptied or put down (for example,
"Folding towels on the sofa, with an empty basket beside them."). Say only what the lines say.
- The chunks follow the record in time order, do not overlap, and together cover every
line. Use only times that appear in the record.

# Output (JSON only, no markdown, no commentary)
{"chunks": [{"start": "HH:MM:SS", "end": "HH:MM:SS", "text": "<one sentence>"}, ...]}
